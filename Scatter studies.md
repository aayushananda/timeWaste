# Four ways to scatter cards over big type

Same structural idea as your reference — oversized word, tilted cards, labels that break the card edge — but each study swaps one ingredient so none of them reads as a copy. Hover or drag a cursor across each stage.

Study 01

## Fanned hand

Cards pivot from one hidden point below the frame instead of sitting where they were dropped. At rest it's a tight closed fan; on hover the hand opens and a single card straightens to the front.

Study 02

## Letterform window

Invert the layers. The cards go flat and quiet — outlines only — and all the texture moves inside the letters, which become a window onto a drifting pattern. The type carries the colour instead of the cards.

Study 03

## Pointer depth

Three depth planes with the word sitting in the middle of the stack, so some cards float in front of it and others are pinned behind. Everything slides against the cursor at different rates — the scatter stops being a flat collage.

Study 04

## Ribbon pass-through

Nothing is placed by hand. A continuous belt of cards drifts behind a fixed word, so cards keep disappearing into the counters of the letters and reappearing on the other side. Hover anywhere to hold it still.
